Convert a curated corpus to immutable little-endian u16 token shards

Usage: rust-llm-pretrain.exe tokenize [OPTIONS] --corpus <CORPUS> --tokenizer <TOKENIZER> --output-dir <OUTPUT_DIR>

Options:
      --corpus <CORPUS>          
      --tokenizer <TOKENIZER>    
      --output-dir <OUTPUT_DIR>  
      --config <CONFIG>          
  -h, --help                     Print help
