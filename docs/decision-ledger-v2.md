## Identity and Change Propagation

The P0A acceptance hashes this complete UTF-8/LF file. Any byte change creates a new
ledger identity. Because `SPAN-001` and other artifacts consume contract/ledger hashes,
the P0A implementation must explicitly record every changed seed operand and downstream
identity. Equality of semantic outputs does not authorize reusing a v1 or earlier
prototype identity.
